# Equity campaign page draft

## Campaign introduction
We are developing Supercars.Tours to help owners compare supercar tours and find specialist services with clearer information. We plan to combine transparent tour comparisons with a directory of suppliers serving owners, starting in the UK.

## The problem we are testing
Owners can spend time moving between operator websites, recommendations and specialist groups to work out which experience or supplier suits them. We want to make the differences easier to understand, including how much a tour focuses on driving and how much it focuses on hotels and hospitality.

## Our approach
We plan to publish comparable operator profiles, explain the evidence behind our assessments and make missing information visible. Owners will be able to discover services such as transport, PPF, carbon repair, specialist maintenance and track activities. Paid listings and sponsored positions will be clearly labelled.

## How the business earns revenue
Suppliers would pay for listings, sponsorship, premium visibility and accepted enquiries under defined terms. Commercial payments would not determine editorial scores. The original pricing proposal ranges from €300 to €5,000 for annual listings, with additional packages to be validated through supplier pilots.

## Why we are raising
We intend to raise €700k to €1m, subject to a final costed plan and agreed offer terms. The investment would fund product delivery, accurate content, supplier onboarding, customer acquisition and the team needed to operate the service. Our financial model currently uses an illustrative €850k midpoint.

# Investor questions and proposed answers

## Who is the customer
Owners and active buyers use the guide. Suppliers pay for commercial services. A broad enthusiast audience may help reach, but the business must demonstrate that it attracts people with relevant purchasing needs.

## Why would suppliers pay
The hypothesis is that a specialist audience, accurate category information and measurable enquiries can create value beyond a general listing. Supplier pilots, payments, renewals and accepted lead data must substantiate that hypothesis.

## How does independence work
The proposed policy separates editorial scores from paid placement, publishes the methodology and records conflicts. Implementation, staffing and user understanding need to be tested. Sponsorship does not purchase a favourable review.

## What exists today
The founder has identified supercars.tours as the project website. The current feature set, users, traffic, revenue and partner contracts have not been independently verified in this preparation work. The founder must provide a working demo and evidence of any claimed traction.

## Why this amount
€700k to €1m is the founder's intended range. The final ask should match a detailed budget, credible milestones and an appropriate cash reserve. €850k is the model's planning midpoint. It should not be presented as an approved need or accepted valuation.

## What valuation and equity are offered
Neither is set. For a simple primary share issue, new investor ownership equals new investment divided by the sum of pre money valuation and new investment. Options, convertibles, different share rights and other transactions can change the result.

## Will you sell tours or arrange finance
Tour sales are outside the current plan. Finance and insurance are potential supplier categories, but the actual advertising and referral arrangements require a permissions assessment before activation. The current forecast does not depend on regulated referral income.

## How could investors make a return
Any return depends on future business performance and a lawful distribution or liquidity event, neither of which is assured. No exit timetable, dividend policy, resale facility or return multiple is promised.

## Why start in the UK
Current operations are UK only. Concentrating product and commercial validation in one market limits the initial delivery burden. Italy based involvement and discussions in Ireland and Spain are not evidence of operational launches in those countries.
