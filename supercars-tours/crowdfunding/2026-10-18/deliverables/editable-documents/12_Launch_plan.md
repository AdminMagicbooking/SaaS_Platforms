# Preparation and launch plan

Use this relative timetable once the founder has confirmed availability and budget. It is a suggested work plan, not a promised campaign launch date. Platform assessment, legal work and evidence collection may extend it.

| Period | Work | Completion evidence |
| Weeks 1 and 2 | Confirm issuer, roles, ownership, product status and current finances | Founder input sheet, demo and indexed records |
| Weeks 3 to 6 | Owner and supplier research, category mapping and paid pilot | Interview notes, signed terms, payments and defined metrics |
| Weeks 5 to 8 | Revise model, cost milestones and test investor interest | Cost quotes, downside plan and documented investor feedback |
| Weeks 7 to 10 | Assess platforms and prepare application | Eligibility response, fee quote and required document list |
| After acceptance | Complete diligence, offer terms and approved campaign assets | Platform and adviser requirements satisfied |
| Before public launch | Test campaign demand and confirm delivery resources | Approved materials and credible commitments |
| During campaign | Answer questions and update material changes | Consistent disclosures and current evidence |
| After successful close | Complete legal steps and begin funded delivery | Funds available, cap table and reporting calendar |

## Launch conditions
The issuing entity and authority to raise are confirmed. Product claims are demonstrated. Published traction is traceable to evidence. The financial plan funds the minimum delivery scope and includes material obligations. Team roles, IP ownership and supplier contracts are documented. The selected platform has completed the required review and approved the public materials.

Keep an evidence based commitment register separating interest, nonbinding pledges, signed commitments and cleared funds. Do not invent social proof or describe expressions of interest as secured investment. Agree any public commitment target with the platform; there is no universal threshold assumed here.

## If preparation does not support launch
Extend validation, revise the scope or define a separately funded milestone round. Preserve cash by pausing unproven acquisition spending and unfunded hiring. A successful campaign is not a substitute for supplier demand.

## Reporting after close
Provide updates on product delivery, relevant owner use, paying suppliers, renewals, accepted leads, cash and material risks. Follow the investor documents and platform reporting obligations. State the period, source and explanation for changes. Report delays and corrective actions promptly.
