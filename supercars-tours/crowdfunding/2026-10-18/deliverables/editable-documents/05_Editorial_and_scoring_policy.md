# Editorial independence and scoring proposal

Trust is central to the proposed product. The commercial model should make it possible for an owner to understand how each assessment was produced and whether the supplier paid for visibility.

## Proposed comparison method
Maintain separate driving and luxury dimensions rather than assuming one universal best tour. Publish the criteria, rating scale, assessment date, evidence sources and missing information. Agree the methodology before publishing numeric scores.

For driving, proposed criteria include driving time, route character, group size, support arrangements and flexibility. For luxury, proposed criteria include accommodation, dining, hospitality and included services. Test whether owners find these dimensions useful. A stated hotel star rating or a route description should not automatically become an editorial verdict.

## Evidence and uncertainty
Distinguish operator supplied facts, desk research, direct experience and owner feedback. Never imply that a tour was personally tested when it was researched online. If important information is unavailable, show that limitation instead of assigning an arbitrary score. Publish a correction route and record the reason for each material change.

Owner feedback should include the experience date and a relevant verification basis where practical. Verification is a process description, not a guarantee of truth. Moderate conflicts, fabricated reviews, personal data and supplier responses under a consistent policy.

## Commercial separation
- Clearly label advertisements, category sponsorship and premium positions.
- Keep organic sorting and scores independent of commercial spend.
- Offer a factual correction process to paying and unpaid operators alike.
- Record personal relationships, free trips, gifts, affiliate arrangements and other conflicts.
- Let the editorial owner reject an inaccurate commercial claim.

## Governance and practical tests
Before launch, score the same sample profiles with two reviewers and compare disagreements. Revise ambiguous criteria. Ask owners to complete comparison tasks and explain the meaning of each rating. Keep an evidence folder for published claims and a version history for the methodology.

The board or founder should review complaints, scoring changes involving sponsors and any pressure to change editorial content. Supplier contracts must state that payment has no influence over editorial assessments. This policy is a proposal to implement and test; it does not establish that an independent scoring operation already exists.
