# Financial model and funding discipline

The workbook is a 36 month illustration in euros, beginning in the first fully funded operating month. It is designed to expose the relationship between supplier growth, revenue timing, costs and cash. It is not a valuation, audited forecast or complete set of financial statements.

## Model structure
Summary presents annual recognised revenue, collections, operating results, cash and customer volumes. Assumptions contains editable prices, conversion rates, costs, launch months and monthly activity. Forecast calculates the monthly operating plan. Raise calculates net investment proceeds, first year deployment and simple equity ownership when valuation and share inputs are supplied.

Annual listings and sponsorships are billed at the start of each service year and recognised across 12 months. Renewal occurs at the annual anniversary. Premium placement and accepted leads are recognised and collected monthly. Fractional customer and lead counts represent expected values. Sponsorship inventory and premium placements are capped.

## Inputs to replace before submission
Replace opening cash, existing customers, deferred revenue, liabilities, supplier prices, conversion rates, customer additions, renewals, salaries, development quotes and acquisition costs. The model currently assumes no existing cohorts or opening obligations. This assumption does not establish actual trading history.

All UK cost estimates must be entered as euro equivalents using a documented budget rate and date. No GBP to EUR conversion has been performed in this illustration. Add a sterling cost schedule and currency sensitivity when quotes are available. Confirm whether a UK issuer can raise and settle in euros on the selected platform.

## Material limits
The model assumes immediate collection and excludes VAT, corporation tax, bad debts, debt service, existing liabilities and a detailed balance sheet. Development is treated as cash spending and fully expensed for this plan. Contingency is modelled as additional spending. The operating result shown before development is not net profit.

The final model needs management accounts, tax and working capital schedules, founder remuneration, employment costs and the selected platform's fee basis. Run slower sales, weaker renewal and higher cost cases before committing to the ask. Any spending before the round closes needs a separately funded plan.
