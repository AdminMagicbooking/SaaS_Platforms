# Risk register and proposed mitigations

The following are material preparation risks. Ratings and probabilities are deliberately not assigned without management assessment. Mitigation reduces exposure; it does not eliminate it.

| Risk | Possible effect | Proposed response |
| Weak owner intent | Traffic produces few useful enquiries | Test current owner tasks and measure accepted leads |
| Supplier willingness to pay | Low conversion or renewal | Paid pilots with clear delivery and renewal evidence |
| Editorial conflicts | Loss of owner trust | Published method, labelled advertising and conflict review |
| Search and channel dependence | Acquisition cost or traffic changes | Measure channel contribution and build permission based return use |
| Product or data quality | Inaccurate comparisons and complaints | Source records, update cadence, testing and corrections |
| Delivery and key people | Delays or loss of access | Agreed roles, technical owner, IP terms and controlled account access |
| Lead privacy or permissions | Complaints, enforcement or costs | Review the actual data journey and regulated categories before activation |
| Funding and FX | Delayed close or higher sterling costs | Minimum funded scope, cash reserve and dated currency assumptions |
| Investor liquidity and dilution | Capital loss or reduced ownership | Clear offer terms, risk disclosure and no promised exit |

## Demand and financial downside tests
Reduce new supplier additions, delay the paid launch, reduce annual renewals and increase salaries or development costs. Model slower receipts, disputed leads and GBP cost increases once underlying costs are available. Define when management would stop an experiment, defer a hire or reduce marketing.

## Commercial concentration
Track the share of revenue from the largest suppliers and sponsors. Category sponsorship inventory is limited; it cannot grow indefinitely through the same slots. Check that a premium package provides useful visibility without crowding out ordinary listings or misleading owners.

## Scope and responsibility
Tour and supplier information can affect high value decisions. Keep claims accurate and state the basis of assessment. A listing or comparison must not be presented as a safety, insurance or workmanship guarantee. Have the actual contracts and user journey reviewed rather than relying on a general disclaimer.

Assign an owner, review date and evidence of action to each risk before submission. Add risks specific to the final company structure, employment arrangements, suppliers and investment instrument.

# Data room and company evidence checklist

Prepare a controlled data room with a dated index and access appropriate to the recipient. Share sensitive identity, banking and personal information through the platform's secure process. This list identifies evidence to collect; it does not create or verify the underlying records.

## Company and ownership
- Certificate of incorporation, company number, registered office, group chart and trading names.
- Current articles, statutory registers, beneficial ownership records and shareholder agreements.
- A reconciled cap table covering issued shares, options, warrants, convertibles and other rights.
- Previous investment documents, debt, grants, founder advances and material obligations.
- Authority and consents needed for the proposed round and any new share rights.

## Team and intellectual property
- Full identities, approved biographies, roles, time commitments and director appointments.
- Employment, consultancy and adviser agreements, confidentiality and IP assignments.
- Domain registration and control, code ownership, design and content licences, trademarks and relevant open source obligations.
- A list of disputes, conflicts, related party transactions and any required disclosures.

## Financial and tax evidence
- Historic accounts where available, current management accounts and bank reconciliations.
- Revenue by contract and period, invoices, receipts, deferred revenue and renewal cohorts.
- Liabilities, payroll obligations, taxes, litigation and contingencies.
- Forecast assumptions, cost quotes, hiring plan, currency basis and use of funds.
- Treatment of campaign fees, VAT, corporation tax and potential investor tax relief if relevant.

## Product and commercial evidence
- Current demonstration, feature status, delivery estimates and technical dependencies.
- Analytics exports with metric definitions and data periods.
- Signed supplier contracts, commercial terms, paid pilot results and actual renewals.
- Permissions for logos, testimonials, partner names, photos and quotations.
- Scoring method, sample assessments, review moderation and corrections log.

## Data room controls
Each record should have a document owner, version, date and disclosure status. Redact personal data where the full detail is unnecessary. Keep an index of the exact documents used to substantiate campaign claims. Crowdcube's published process includes checks on the company, directors and claim evidence [S8]; the selected platform will determine its actual requests.

# Offer and operating document requirements

These documents need to be completed or supplied after the legal entity, instrument and platform are known. The preparation drafts in this pack do not replace the executed offer or operating documents.

## Investment documents
| Document | Responsible party to confirm | Dependency |
| Platform application and company disclosures | Founder with platform | Issuer identity and substantiated business information |
| Final pitch and financial promotions | Platform and issuer with advisers | Current evidence and required approval process |
| Subscription or investment agreement | Platform and legal adviser | Instrument, share class, price and closing conditions |
| Articles and shareholder or nominee terms | Legal adviser and platform | Rights, governance and investor structure |
| Board and shareholder approvals | Directors, shareholders and adviser | Company constitution and transaction terms |
| Share issuance and registry filings | Company and adviser | Completed allotment and applicable deadlines |
| Investor risk and disclosure materials | Issuer and platform | Applicable investor and offer jurisdictions |

If an EU crowdfunding offer is used, assess whether the European Crowdfunding Service Providers Regulation applies and whether a key investment information sheet is required. Article 23 sets responsibilities for preparing and checking that information [S9]. A UK operating business is not automatically an eligible EU issuer or offer.

## Operating documents
Prepare website terms, privacy and cookie notices, supplier listing terms, lead sharing terms, sponsorship agreements, review moderation and complaints policies, editorial independence rules and appropriate contractor agreements. Draft each against the actual product, data collection, supplier role and payment flow.

Finance and insurance need an activity specific assessment before enquiries or commissions are enabled [S6, S7]. For email and WhatsApp marketing, document the recipient type, permission or other applicable route, suppression process and evidence of sign up [S5].

## Tax and currency decisions
Confirm the UK entity's accounting currency, proposed euro denomination and settlement, sterling operating costs, tax treatment and bank fees. Do not promise SEIS or EIS availability or other investor tax outcomes without confirming eligibility and the permitted campaign wording.

The founder should appoint the appropriate legal and accounting support once the platform and company structure are selected. Record unresolved items, owners and evidence of completion before submission.
