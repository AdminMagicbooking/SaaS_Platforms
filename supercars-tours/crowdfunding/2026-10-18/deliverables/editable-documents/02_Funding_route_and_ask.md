# Recommended funding route

Equity crowdfunding is the strongest fit for the intended €700k to €1m raise. Supercars.Tours is seeking capital for a continuing commercial platform, and shares give backers an interest in the business. This recommendation concerns the funding structure; it does not establish that the business can yet raise that amount.

## Recommended sequence
1. Establish the issuing company, ownership and intellectual property rights.
2. Run a focused UK commercial pilot and collect evidence of owner use and supplier payments.
3. Test the investment case with a suitable lead investor and seek documented commitments on agreed terms.
4. Apply to an equity platform with a substantiated pitch, model and launch community.
5. Launch only when the platform's requirements, offer documents and sufficient campaign demand are in place.

If the pilot evidence is too early for the full round, define a smaller milestone round with its own budget. Do not reduce the target arbitrarily or promise that a subsequent raise will occur. Any staged round must fund a usable next milestone independently.

## Platform assessment
| Candidate | Relevant published criteria | Next decision |
| Crowdcube | Its partner guidance describes growth capital for private companies and ideally revenue with customer or user traction [S1] | Confirm UK issuer eligibility, euro denomination and evidence expectations |
| Republic Europe | Eligibility includes UK, EU, EEA or Swiss business and community presence, a target above £/€150k and appropriate community and precommitments [S2] | Confirm UK issuer structure, euro raise and launch support |

Neither platform has assessed or accepted Supercars.Tours. Request current fee quotes, required legal documents, minimum commitments, nominee arrangements and investor country restrictions. A UK operating footprint does not establish the company's registration or permission to market securities globally.

## Other funding routes
Rewards may help test demand for a defined guide or membership benefit, but are a weak primary fit for this round size and supplier funded model. Kickstarter currently prohibits equity and certain service provider or marketplace projects; the proposed platform would require careful eligibility assessment [S3]. Debt requires credible repayment capacity that has not yet been demonstrated.

UK investment and loan crowdfunding sit within FCA regulated categories. Rewards and donation routes are treated differently [S4]. Use the selected platform and advisers to determine the actual offer and promotion requirements.

# Funding ask and use of proceeds

The founder's intended range is €700,000 to €1,000,000. Use €850,000 only to plan delivery and assess affordability until the budget, valuation and investor demand support a final target. Euro fundraising and UK operations require an explicit policy for sterling costs and currency risk.

## Illustrative gross and net proceeds
The following arithmetic assumes an 8 percent variable fundraising allowance and €20,000 of fixed fundraising and legal costs. These are planning assumptions, not platform quotations. They exclude any additional taxes on fees unless included in the final quotes.

## Spending proposal to cost
Build the initial comparison and directory product, maintain supplier information and scoring evidence, recruit the delivery team, acquire relevant UK users and test paid supplier packages. Budget separately for privacy, contracts, accounting, insurance and campaign execution. Retain a cash reserve for delays and weaker collections.

The financial workbook links its first year spending categories to the monthly forecast. Its assumptions do not represent supplier quotes, salary commitments or an approved hiring plan. Any excess of the target over the model's funding need must be justified by specific additional activity or retained capital policy. Do not manufacture costs to match the desired raise.

## Scope at different amounts
- At €700k, prioritise the UK website, evidence quality, supplier onboarding and one repeatable acquisition process. Release hiring and marketing funds against measured results.
- At €850k, add capacity only where the pilot demonstrates a bottleneck and the revised model supports the hire or channel.
- At €1m, additional product or market work needs its own budget and acceptance criteria. International expansion and a native app are not automatic commitments.

## Terms still required
Confirm the minimum close, maximum accepted investment, valuation, share class, shareholder rights, option pool, existing convertibles, fees, use of any overfunding and treatment of a failed campaign. No investor return, exit date or tax relief is promised in this pack.
