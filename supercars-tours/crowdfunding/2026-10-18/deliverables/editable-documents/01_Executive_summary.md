# Executive summary

Supercars.Tours is developing a guide that helps supercar owners compare tour operators and find specialist services. The proposed business earns revenue from suppliers seeking visibility and enquiries from owners. We intend to preserve editorial independence through published scoring rules and clear advertising labels.

## The customer problem
The founding hypothesis is that owners find it difficult to compare driving tours and assess specialist suppliers across scattered websites, recommendations and social groups. A tour focused on driving can offer a very different experience from one focused on hotels and hospitality. Owners need those differences explained in comparable terms.

## The proposed product
The initial product combines operator profiles, driving and luxury assessments, comparison filters and a directory of specialist suppliers. Owner feedback, newsletters and enquiry routing can follow once the underlying data and consent processes work. Native mobile apps, route planning and community features are later options, conditional on repeat use and funding.

## Who pays
The initial commercial focus is UK suppliers in transport, PPF and detailing, carbon repair, exhausts and parts, specialist maintenance, classic car repair and track activities. Finance and insurance remain potential categories, subject to review of the actual advertising and referral activity before activation.

The original proposed annual listing range is €300 to €5,000. Sponsorship is €5,000 to €10,000 annually, leads €20 to €80 each and premium placement €500 to €1,500 monthly. These are proposed prices, with no willingness to pay evidence yet supplied.

## Funding and team
The intended raise is €700k to €1m. We recommend an equity route, supported by a lead investor and a community campaign once evidence supports the amount. The founder, Charlie as COO and Lorenzo Granay are the stated team. Roles, time commitments, biographies and ownership need documentary confirmation.

## What needs to be demonstrated
The investment case depends on a relevant audience, supplier willingness to pay, reliable lead attribution, repeat supplier revenue and an editorial process that resists commercial influence. No revenue, traffic, registered user count, signed partners or historic financial statements have been supplied. Those figures must be added with dated evidence before this summary becomes an investor document.
