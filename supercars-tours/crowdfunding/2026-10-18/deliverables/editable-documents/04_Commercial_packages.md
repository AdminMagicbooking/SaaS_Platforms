# Supplier pricing and commercial terms brief

The following structure turns the proposed revenue streams into packages that can be tested with UK suppliers. All amounts are proposed in euros. Confirm customer billing currency, VAT treatment and tax inclusive or exclusive presentation before quoting or contracting.

| Offer | Original proposed price | Deliverable to define |
| Annual listing | €300 to €5,000 per year | Profile content, categories, locations, reporting and support |
| Category sponsorship | €5,000 to €10,000 per year | Named inventory, period, permitted creative and exclusivity scope |
| Accepted lead | €20 to €80 per lead | Eligibility, attribution, duplicates, acceptance and refunds |
| Premium placement | €500 to €1,500 per month | Labelled position, availability, delivery period and reporting |

## Suggested pilot offer
Test a straightforward annual listing with a fixed profile specification and transparent renewal terms. Add higher priced packages only when the team can demonstrate incremental commercial value. Do not bundle promised lead volumes into a listing unless the cost, delivery obligation and refund treatment are explicitly agreed.

The model uses an illustrative €900 blended annual listing price, €7,500 sponsorship, €750 monthly premium placement and €40 per accepted lead. These are modelling assumptions within the original ranges, not accepted customer prices.

## Lead acceptance specification
A billable lead should identify the requested service, relevant location and a usable contact method. The owner must understand which named supplier will receive the enquiry. Define how long a duplicate remains a duplicate, when a lead is too old, how the supplier disputes a charge and when a credit is issued. A contact detail alone is not a sale or a completed job.

## Agreement drafting brief
Include the contracting entity, package, delivery scope, term, fees, payment schedule, currency, tax basis, renewal notice, cancellation, suspension, complaint handling, liability allocation and data responsibilities. Obtain permission for logos, images and testimonials. Define analytics access and distinguish clicks, enquiries and accepted leads.

Finance and insurance referral activity should remain inactive until the exact user journey and commercial arrangements have been assessed. An advertising label or description as a lead generator does not settle whether an activity needs permission. Relevant FCA guidance addresses credit brokers and insurance introductions [S6, S7].

Sponsorship and premium placement buy disclosed visibility. They do not buy an editorial score, a favourable owner review or a guarantee of supplier quality.
