# Supercars Tours crowdfunding preparation pack

We are preparing an equity funding round of €700,000 to €1,000,000 for Supercars.Tours, an independent guide and supplier discovery platform for supercar owners. Current operations are UK only. The working financial model uses €850,000 as a planning midpoint, not an agreed investment offer.

The immediate task is to demonstrate that suppliers will pay for access to relevant owners and that owners trust the comparisons. The final funding target should follow a costed delivery plan and evidence of demand.

## What is established
- The founder has specified an intended €700k to €1m raise and UK operations.
- The proposed team includes the founder, Charlie as COO and Lorenzo Granay in Italy. Lorenzo's role and all legal appointments remain to be confirmed.
- Discussions are in progress with one person in Ireland and one in Spain. They are not confirmed hires or country launches.
- Proposed revenue comes from supplier listings, category sponsorship, lead generation and premium placement. The platform does not sell tours.

## How to use the pack
Read the executive summary and funding recommendation first. Complete the founder input sheet, validate the model and assemble the evidence register before circulating an investment pitch. The campaign copy, pitch outline and outreach messages are starting drafts.

The PDF combines the documents for review. Matching Markdown files are editable sources. The Excel workbook contains a 36 month operating and cash forecast, funding calculations and reconciliation checks. The slide outline is content for a future deck, not a completed presentation.

## Submission status
Not ready for submission. Incorporation details, ownership, current financials, product evidence, traction, founder biographies, valuation and investment terms remain unconfirmed. Platform acceptance and required legal documents depend on the issuer, instrument and investor jurisdictions. The checklist is a preparation register, not a complete statutory filing pack.

The contents page lists each document and its starting page. All sources were checked for preparation on 18 October 2026.
