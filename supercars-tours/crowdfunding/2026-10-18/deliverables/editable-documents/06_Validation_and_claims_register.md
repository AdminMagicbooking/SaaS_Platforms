# Validation plan and investor evidence register

The pitch needs evidence of both owner demand and supplier willingness to pay. The proposed research below is a practical starting point, not a platform rule or a statistical guarantee.

## Proposed pilot
Interview 20 UK owners or active buyers and 20 suppliers across the selected categories. Ask about recent decisions and actual spending before presenting the product. Then run a small paid pilot with suppliers on clear terms. A suggested initial objective is five paying suppliers and a complete account of enquiries delivered, accepted and rejected. These are future research targets, not achieved traction.

## Owner interview questions
1. How did you choose your last tour or specialist supplier?
2. What evidence changed your decision, and what information was missing?
3. Which driving and hospitality differences matter to you?
4. Use the prototype for a current decision. Observe the task.
5. What would make you distrust sponsored content or comparisons?

## Supplier interview questions
1. Which customers do you serve, and what makes an enquiry useful?
2. What do you spend on acquisition, and how is it measured?
3. What makes a lead invalid, and who decides whether to pay?
4. Which package would you buy, at what price and on what terms?
5. Will you agree to a defined paid pilot?

## Evidence register fields
Record a claim ID, exact wording, location, evidence file, period, method, owner, verification date and publication permission. Label claims as verified, founder supplied, proposed, forecast or unsupported. Nonbinding interest is not revenue or a contract.

| Claim to establish | Evidence | Current status |
| Active user demand | Dated analytics with definitions and bot filtering | Not supplied |
| Paying suppliers | Executed contracts, invoices and matching receipts | Not supplied |
| Repeat commercial value | Renewals, accepted leads and supplier feedback | Not supplied |
| Product functionality | Working demo and dated feature inventory | Not independently verified |
| Team capability | Approved biographies, references and agreed roles | Partial founder description |

## Monthly traction report template
Reporting period: [START AND END]   Prepared by: [NAME]   Evidence folder: [LINK]

Record website sessions, defined active users, verified owner share if measured, newsletter subscribers with permission, and repeat usage. State the definitions and any measurement changes.

Record supplier prospects contacted, proposals, executed contracts, active paying suppliers, invoiced revenue, cash received, cancellations and renewals. Distinguish pipeline value from contracted revenue.

Record enquiries, valid enquiries, accepted paid leads, rejected leads by reason, disputes, credits and attributable supplier outcomes where available. Protect owner personal data in investor materials.

Record acquisition spend, sales effort, moderation time and delivery costs. Explain whether improvements result from a new channel, seasonality, a definition change or a repeatable process.

The report should end with a decision: continue, change the offer, revise the acquisition channel or stop the test. Keep unsuccessful results in the internal record so the forecast reflects actual learning.
