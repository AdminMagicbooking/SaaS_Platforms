Executive summary

Read the honest version first

Coreproma proposes to design, build, host and support the platform Ethical Power refers to as the Ethical Estimation Engine. It captures a project through a guided wizard, quantifies its risks as named budget provisions, generates a bill of materials and cost estimate from a controlled rate library, and reports risk and cost exposure to management and investors. Delivery is phased. Phase 1, to end 2026: the estimating core plus the delivery and governance workspace, the latter not bespoke development at all but Coreproma's existing, field-proven Jobs Tracker platform, extended for solar project delivery and sold as a per-user monthly subscription. Go-live mid-December, followed by a January hypercare window. Phase 2, Q1 2027: supplier and third-party management, full management and investor reporting, and rate intelligence.

The extra weeks to December are not slack: scope moved in with the dateMoving go-live from mid-November to mid-December buys about four weeks. Phase 1 now also has to deliver the Jobs Tracker solar projects extension and the governance integration, which the November plan deferred. Net float is close to zero, and the date holds only if the contract is signed by end August. UAT and cut-over cannot run in the week of 25 December: mid-December is the real freeze, and January is hypercare, not build.

Scope of development · the estimating core

Phase 1 · live mid-December

B1 · Solar Farm Creation Wizard

A guided, ten-step wizard for a commercial user, not an engineer. Live cost panel and confidence flags from step 4.

  • Free-issue scope captured first: what the client supplies vs what the EPC installs can halve the apparent EPC cost
  • GEOALT terrain upload: site area, slope, terrain class, interactive 3D viewer
  • Scaling engine derives module, string, inverter and station counts; PVcase import replaces derived quantities with design data
  • Save-draft, per-step validation, locked outputs with revisioning

38 person-days · Phase 1

B2 · Project Validation

Converts wizard output into a formal, governed project record before anything can progress.

  • Draft promoted to a locked record with an auditable revision history
  • Automated feasibility and compliance checks with a mandatory acknowledgement gate
  • Technical sign-off routed to the responsible role

6 person-days · Phase 1

B2b · Risk Register & Provisions

The differentiator: every risk is priced as a named £ provision, not a blanket percentage, and flows into the budget as a visible line.

  • Per-risk capture: category, status, likelihood, named £ provision, basis of estimate
  • Categories: ground, information gaps, grid connection, planning, access and environment
  • Confidence score dial with rule-based deductions

10 person-days · Phase 1

B3 · Product & Stock Calculator

The core engine: auto-generated bill of materials, priced from the rate library across 17 cost packages.

  • Point-in-time rates, territory-specific, confidence flags, role-based ownership
  • Free-issue deduction: supply cost zeroed, installation retained, both shown
  • Location, ground, access and programme multipliers; inflation; contingency and margin
  • Outputs: budget summary, full BOQ to Excel and PDF, assumptions register, sensitivity table, investor one-pager

38 person-days incl. exports · Phase 1

Every user completes multi-factor authentication on sign-in, enforced by a Conditional Access policy.

The proposed commercial structure

One route, capped

Platforms of this kind change while they are being built: rate structures move, outputs get reshaped once real tenders run through them, and the first live estimates always suggest improvements. Rather than quote a range and invoice the difference, Coreproma proposes a single route. A new jointly-held structure owns the platform IP, and in exchange the development cost is capped. Currency GBP.

Shared ownership, capped development

Hard cap on Phase 1 development, including six months of Azure hosting from go-live

  • Six months of hosting from go-live is included, covering the period in which development continues. Azure is billed at cost thereafter

Ownership of the platform

One structure, jointly held

Coreproma proposes a single ownership route rather than a menu. A new jointly-held structure owns the intellectual property in the developed estimation platform, and that shared ownership is what makes the capped price possible. Ethical Power's own data, its rate library figures, projects and estimates, always belongs to Ethical Power regardless of structure.

What the structure holds

A new entity, jointly held by Ethical Power and Coreproma, owns the IP in the bespoke estimation platform.

  • The developed modules: creation wizard, validation, risk register, cost calculator, exports, and the Q1 2027 supplier and reporting work
  • Neither party can commercialise the platform unilaterally; decisions sit with the structure
  • Coreproma's pre-existing background IP and generic components are contributed or licensed in, and must be separated from the newly written foreground code in the contract
  • Third-party licences, for example PVcase and mapping, are never assignable by Coreproma

What each side is trading

The structure is the consideration for the cap. Both sides give something up.

  • Ethical Power gains cost certainty at £250,000 and a share of an asset it helped specify, instead of exclusive ownership of the code
  • Coreproma absorbs build cost above the cap and gives up the right to resell the platform freely, in exchange for a continuing interest in it
  • Still to be agreed: shareholdings, what each party contributes, governance and deadlock, what happens on exit, and how the EcoGrid Resort software sits inside the same structure
⚑This is a corporate transaction, not a payment term. None of the terms in the last bullet above are agreed, and they cannot be settled inside a development contract. This section is offered as heads of terms to be negotiated in parallel with the build contract, not as a settled deal. Because it is now the only route proposed, drafting those heads of terms sits on the critical path to signature, not after it.

Annex A · Proposed additional modules

Indicative only · not priced
⚑This annex is not part of the quotation. Neither module is scoped, estimated or priced, neither is included in Phase 1 or in the Q1 2027 work, and nothing here forms part of what Ethical Power is being asked to approve. Both are recorded so the direction is visible early. If either is wanted, it is scoped and quoted separately, after the estimating core is live.

Module 1 · Investor portal

Projects carrying third-party investors, as with the French project, need those investors to see the documents relevant to an investment decision and the reports produced as the project proceeds.

  • Cheaper than it looks: the platform already produces what an investor needs, the named risk provisions, the budget and sensitivity table, the investor one-pager and live cost exposure. The portal is a permissioned external role over existing data plus a document register, not a second application
  • Least privilege by project: an investor sees only the project or SPV they are in
  • A hard boundary around what never leaves: tender pricing, margin and the rate library
  • Watermarking and an access log, giving an immutable record of what was disclosed and when, which is the part that matters in a dispute

Module 2 · Site capture

Mission-planned drone capture of a site, with the imagery landing on the project record: terrain for the wizard, dated evidence against risk provisions, and progress for reporting.

  • Planning software only, on Coreproma's existing WaypointsCreator platform: survey grids, 3D terrain simulation, obstacle detection and mission export to DJI controllers
  • Imagery and orthomosaics attach to the project record and feed the Step 2 terrain model, replacing a separate topographic survey upload
  • Flight operations are excluded, deliberately. Commercial site survey flying in the United Kingdom is a Specific category activity: it needs a CAA Operational Authorisation, a certified remote pilot and third-party insurance under EC 785/2004. Those obligations sit with whoever flies, Ethical Power or its surveyor, not with Coreproma
  • Commercial basis would be the existing WaypointsCreator per-seat subscription plus integration work
