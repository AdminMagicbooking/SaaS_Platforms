01

What gets built, and when

Two phases

Phase 1 · live mid-December 2026

The estimating core, plus the workspace that runs delivery. Signature is needed by end August to hold the date.

  • Creation wizard · ten guided steps, free-issue scope, terrain upload, quantities derived from capacity
  • Validation · the draft becomes a locked, auditable project record with sign-off
  • Risk register · every risk priced as a named £ provision that lands in the budget as a visible line
  • Cost calculator · bill of materials across 17 packages, BOQ to Excel and PDF, sensitivity table, investor one-pager
  • Delivery & governance workspace · phases, tasks and provision tracking, on Coreproma's existing Jobs Tracker rather than newly built

Phase 2 · Q1 2027

Procurement and intelligence, once the core is live and real tenders have run through it.

  • Supplier & third-party management · offers against the bill of materials scored on price, lead time and ESG, plus supplier onboarding, pre-qualification and compliance documents
  • Full management & investor reporting · live risk and cost exposure, scheduled weekly and monthly reports
  • Rate intelligence · supplier quotes and commodity indices keeping the rate library current

03

Shared ownership, capped development

The proposed structure
£250,000
Hard cap on Phase 1 development, including six months of Azure hosting from go-live
  • Conditional on a new jointly-held structure owning the platform IP. Comparable builds of this scope are normally open-ended, with no ceiling on the final cost; this one has a ceiling, and Coreproma carries everything above it
  • Ethical Power gains cost certainty and a share of the platform, instead of exclusive ownership of the code
  • Jobs Tracker seats are a separate monthly subscription, outside the cap; Azure is billed at cost after the first six months
  • The same capped approach is offered for the EcoGrid Resort software, scope to be defined separately
