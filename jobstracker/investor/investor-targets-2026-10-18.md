# Jobs Tracker — investor target list (pre-seed £500k–£1m into Workwiz Limited)

Date: 18 October 2026 · Researched and verified against each investor's own site or a credible report; anything marked *unverified* was not confirmed. Workwiz is a 2003 company: not SEIS/EIS eligible, and also outside age-capped start-up schemes (Malta Enterprise ≤5 years, Enterprise Ireland HPSU). Scoring reflects that.

## Three facts that shaped the list

1. **Non-SEIS kills most UK angel syndicates and all SEIS/EIS fund managers.** The realistic UK targets are institutional pre-seed funds (no tax-relief dependence) and the two or three syndicates that do non-EIS deals.
2. **£36k ARR (~£3k MRR) is below every revenue-based-financing threshold** (Uncapped, Wayflyer, Outfund: ~£10k MRR; re:cap: €250k ARR). Revisit RBF when MRR passes £10k — on the plan that is month 5–6 after close.
3. **The trades-software comparables were not VC-funded at pre-seed.** Commusoft (no external money found), BigChange (PE at £100m), Joblogic (growth equity), Tradify/Fergus (NZ/AU funds), Payaca (Blackfinch EIS, £331k). "Who backed X" leads to PE; the real audience is construction-tech pre-seed funds and non-EIS UK generalists.

## Do not bother

- **SyndicateRoom, Portfolio Ventures, Fuel, Haatch, Blackfinch, Envestors** — SEIS/EIS is the vehicle. Not fixable by pitching.
- **All RBF** until MRR passes £10k.
- **Enterprise Ireland, Elkstone, Malta Enterprise** — need an Irish start-up or a company ≤5 years old.
- **Builders merchants, telematics vendors, trade bodies, Checkatrade** — no venture activity; pursue as channel partners.
- **Brick & Mortar, Trimble, Nauta, Frontline (today)** — right sector, wrong cheque or thesis now; keep warm for the seed.
- **Backers of the comparables (Great Hill, Axiom, Vista, Movac, K1)** — growth/PE.

## Sequencing

1. Week 1: warm intros from your own network (MagicBooking, Malta, Paris) — these convert; cold applications rarely do.
2. Week 1–2: Playfair, Foundamental, Pi Labs, SuperSeed, Zacua — one tailored e-mail each with the deck PDF and the model; mention Workwiz/non-EIS in the e-mail, not in a follow-up.
3. Week 2–3: Hilti Venture, Concrete, Paris Business Angels, LBAN, Cambridge Angels/24Haymarket.
4. After Gaswise signs and the first CampaignBuilder send has numbers: refresh the traction slide and go back to anyone who said "too early".
