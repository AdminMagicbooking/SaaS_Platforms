## Objective
A worker-focused companion to the office-manager film. Show Sam managing a complete working day from the phone. Retain navy/lime/teal branding, light workplace humour, the younger Archie voice and background music. Format: 1920x1080, 24 fps, exactly 60 seconds.

## Narration
Meet Sam. One phone. A whole working day.

Sign in with biometrics. Check today’s jobs, upcoming work and push alerts.

Read and sign RAMS, then start travel with Google Maps or Waze.

Arrived? GPS tap-in. Follow the checklist and add photos, notes or a message to the office.

Log materials, delivery notes, extra work and purchase order numbers while you’re there.

Capture completion photos and the client’s signature. Tap out. Done.

Check and submit your weekly timesheet. No Friday paperwork treasure hunt.

Request leave, show training certificates, and receive expiry alerts.

Van checks and defect reports? There too, when enabled.

Jobs Tracker. Your working day, in your pocket. iPhone and Android. Jobs tracker dot work.

## Claims and presentation
Do not imply workers approve their own payroll or that timesheets bypass office approval. Do not imply logging a PO automatically orders a part. Show vehicle checks/defects as “when enabled”. Safety documents are signed before work proceeds. No invented productivity percentage or offline guarantee. Use a fictional job, site and worker. Phone UI should have large readable controls; animate actual actions and confirmations rather than presenting a static feature list.
