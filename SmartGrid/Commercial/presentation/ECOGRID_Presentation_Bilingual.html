1 What is the offer

An energy operating system for outdoor hospitality

ECOGRID RESORT™ orchestrates a site's energy in real time — maximising solar self-consumption, cutting grid peaks and protecting guest comfort — sold as software plus integrated assets, deployed site by site and then as a European network.

The thesis — the edge is distribution, not hardware. Solar and EMS hardware are commodities (FoxESS, SolarEdge, Huawei, SMA, Victron). The defensible advantage is existing access to operators through COREPROMA, the tourism-vertical software on top, and COREPROMA occupancy data feeding AI load forecasting — something no generic EMS has. Long-term value comes from recurring software and the network of connected sites, not equipment sales.

Source: ECOGRID_Business_Plan_EN.docx §1–3; brain.md §0, §1.2; SmartGridResort_Master_2026-06_v2.docx.

1 Why now

The customer pain — and three regulatory tailwinds

⚡

Operator pain points

Volatile electricity bills (A/C, pools, catering, hot water, lighting); high subscribed power and grid-connection constraints in tourist zones; rising guest EV-charging demand; pressure for visible, measurable low-carbon proof; and the administrative complexity of permits and aid applications.

📜

Regulatory drivers (verified June 2026)

EPBD (EU/2024/1275): solar mandatory on existing non-residential >500 m² at major renovation (by 31/12/2027); new >250 m² from end-2026.
APER law (FR): solar carports on car parks >1,500 m² (2026/2028 — softened 2025, under legal challenge; use with caution).
End of FR self-consumption premium (decree 1 June 2026): surplus feed-in cut to €0.011/kWh — a self-consumed kWh is now worth ~20× an exported one, exactly what the EMS + storage maximise.

Source: BP §2; BP_research_verification_2026-06.md §3–7.

1 Pricing & model

Two ways to buy — recurring revenue either way

Model 1 — Direct sale

Client pays CAPEX · keeps 100% of savings
  • Client buys the plant (pays the quote); keeps 100% of the bill reduction
  • SmartGrid earns an EPC margin ≈ 15% on installation (≈ €855k on the reference site)
  • SaaS €5k/yr — all updates & support included
  • Maintenance €10k/yr base (size-based tiers in progress)
  • Client payback ≈ 6–7 yrs on ≈ €860k/yr savings

Model 2 — Financed & savings share

Zero client investment · Energy-as-a-Service
  • SmartGrid finances, installs and owns the plant — €0 for the operator
  • 60% SmartGrid / 40% client savings split, 15-year contract
  • SaaS still sold; maintenance borne by SmartGrid; 30-year land/roof lease
  • Plants sit in asset SPVs (equity + green debt + aids), off the opco balance sheet
  • Volume option: become an aggregator / VPP — pure upside, not in projections

Proposed size-based tiers (to validate): SaaS/maintenance — S €2.5k/€4k · M €4k/€7k · L €5k/€10k (ref) · XL €7k/€15k. Source: BP §5; BP_interview_notes.md §5; Financial Model (Assumptions, Site Economics).

3 Setup & onboarding

How a site gets connected

From first contact to live operation. The deployment funnel (Master v2 §5 "How it works") plus the indicative timeline from the grant-application template (§2).

Qualification

Satellite analysis & invoice extraction

Site solar potential assessed from satellite imagery; 12 months of energy bills parsed (OCR/NLP). A bottom-up consumption model (equipment × hours × season × occupancy) is collectable in a single sales call.

Design

Energy matrix & solar/battery sizing

Loads mapped (pools, restaurant, mobile homes, EV); PV, storage and charger capacity sized from the matrix. Tier and energy-intensity overlay set the offer (Model 1 vs Model 2).

Months 1–2

Engineering study

Detailed design, supplier quotes and a confirmed financial plan (CAPEX/OPEX/savings/payback) exported from the ECOGRID financial model.

Months 2–4

Permits & grid connection

Permit (DP) support module; grid-connection request; aid applications pre-filled by the grant-optimisation engine and run by the in-house grants specialist. Model 2: secure the land/roof lease (≈30-yr).

Months 4–8

Installation

Carports/roofs, inverters, LFP batteries, EV chargers, smart meters and sensors installed and integrated (Modbus / OCPP / MQTT, multi-brand).

Month 9

Commissioning & 24/7 AI operation

EMS goes live; operator dashboard and guest app activated; ESG/carbon reporting enabled; continuous AI optimisation on weather + occupancy, with 24/7 supervision.

Required from the client to start (grant template §10 attachments). Company documents & national ID/SIRET · 12 months of energy bills · site map / satellite view · and — for Model 2 — a signed land-lease agreement.

Source: Master v2 §2 (grant template, timeline & attachments), §5 (How it works), §1 (services).
