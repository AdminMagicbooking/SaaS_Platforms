1Executive Summary

ECOGRID RESORT™ is a technology platform developed by Performance ENR to support the energy transition of campsites, holiday villages and resorts. The goal is to transform every establishment into an intelligent energy micro-grid capable of producing, storing, controlling and optimizing its energy.

The solution combines engineering, integration, works, the proprietary ECOGRID OS™ software, supervision, maintenance and continuous optimization. It addresses the current fragmentation of energy equipment: solar PV, batteries, EV charging stations, building management systems (BMS), pools, pumps, air conditioning and meters still too often operate in silos.

Three revenue engines

  • Engine 1 – Integration & Smart Grid Works: €300k excl. VAT per site, 35% target gross margin, activity concentrated in Q1 and Q4.
  • Engine 2 – ECOGRID OS™: annual license of €3,000 excl. VAT per site, high-margin recurring revenue.
  • Engine 3 – Operations & Maintenance: annual contract of €1,000 excl. VAT per site, supervision, after-sales service and continuous optimization.

2Findings

Campsites are moving upmarket: air-conditioned mobile homes, swimming pools, restaurants, spas, EV charging stations, connected equipment and digital services are sharply increasing consumption. Energy is becoming a factor of competitiveness rather than a simple cost.

  • Equipment is controlled separately through manufacturer portals.
  • Data is scattered across inverters, batteries, charging stations, BMS, meters and internal tools.
  • Self-consumption remains limited due to a lack of overall orchestration.
  • Batteries, pumps, pools and charging stations are not optimized together.
  • Maintenance remains mostly reactive.

This fragmentation creates an opportunity: moving from an equipment-based logic to an energy operating system logic.

4Target Market

Performance ENR's strategy is selective: target first the establishments where the value created is strongest and where the return on investment is most readily demonstrated.

  • 4-star and 5-star campsites: several hundred pitches, pools, restaurants, air conditioning and technical equipment.
  • Holiday villages: multiple buildings, distributed energy uses and a need for centralized supervision.
  • Tourist resorts: high requirement for continuity of service and strong environmental image.
  • Multi-site groups: need for standardization, consolidated reporting and centralized supervision.

5Vision

Performance ENR's vision is to build the European reference for Smart Grids in outdoor hospitality. Energy will become a lever of competitiveness as important as the quality of accommodation and services.

ECOGRID RESORT™ enables operators to regain control of their energy: produce locally, maximize self-consumption, reduce costs, intelligently control usage and simplify technical operations.

In the long term, campsites will evolve into intelligent energy micro-grids integrating solar PV production, batteries, charging stations, HVAC, hot water, pumps, lighting and operational data. The value will lie in the orchestration of these assets by ECOGRID OS™.

6The Solution ECOGRID RESORT™

ECOGRID RESORT™ is an integrated solution designed to control the energy of campsites and holiday villages. It rests on four pillars: produce, store, consume intelligently and optimize.

PillarDescription
ProduceRooftop solar PV, carports, local installations and self-consumption.
StoreBatteries controlled according to production, consumption, weather and priorities.
ConsumeControl of pools, pumps, HVAC, hot water, lighting, EV charging stations.
OptimizeAlgorithms, alerts, predictive maintenance, reporting and energy arbitrage.

The offer covers the entire life cycle: energy audit, engineering, works execution, digitalization via ECOGRID OS™, operations, maintenance and continuous optimization.

7Turnkey Offer

Performance ENR replaces the coordination of multiple parties with a single point of contact responsible for the design, execution and follow-up of the project.

  1. Energy audit: analysis of bills, power ratings, usage and solar potential.
  2. Studies and engineering: sizing of PV, batteries, charging stations, Smart Grid architecture and ROI.
  3. Works: supply, installation, integration, testing and commissioning.
  4. ECOGRID OS™ integration: connectors, dashboards, alerts, scenarios.
  5. Training: onboarding of operations teams.
  6. Operations and maintenance: supervision, after-sales service, updates, optimization.

8ECOGRID OS™

ECOGRID OS™ is the energy operating system for outdoor hospitality. It supervises, controls and optimizes all of a site's energy infrastructure from a single interface.

  • Real-time supervision: production, consumption, batteries, charging stations, alarms.
  • Energy control: batteries, charging stations, pumps, HVAC, hot water, lighting.
  • Self-consumption optimization: storage, arbitrage, usage priorities.
  • Predictive maintenance: detecting drift before failure.
  • Energy and ESG reporting: savings, CO2 avoided, self-consumption rate.
  • Multi-site management for campsite groups.

The open architecture targets integration with leading manufacturers: Huawei, SMA, Sungrow, GoodWe, Fronius, SolarEdge, Schneider, ABB, Socomec, Siemens and other compatible manufacturers.
