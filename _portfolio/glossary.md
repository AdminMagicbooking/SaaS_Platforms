## Methodology & framework vocabulary

- **BMAD** — phased product-development methodology referenced in
  FindAllProperty's spec ("BMAD Phase 1 (Analysis) complete"). Stands for
  Brief / Market / Architecture / Delivery (TODO: confirm exact expansion
  and document the phase definitions if we adopt it as a portfolio standard).
- **OGL** — UK Open Government Licence. Governs the public datasets
  FindAllProperty relies on.
- **WPML** — Waypoint Mission Markup Language (DJI's mission file format,
  used by WaypointsCreator).
- **RAMS** — Risk Assessment and Method Statement (UK construction H&S
  document, used by Jobs Tracker).
- **PV** — Procès-Verbal (French construction site-meeting minutes,
  generated by COREPROMA).
- **EPC** — ⚠ two meanings in this portfolio, always disambiguate:
  1. *Energy Performance Certificate* (UK property energy rating, used by
     FindAllProperty and COREPROMA).
  2. *Engineering, Procurement & Construction* (contractor model — Ethical
     Power is an EPC contractor; the Ethical Estimation Engine and EcoGrid
     docs use this sense).
- **BOQ** — Bill of Quantities (line-by-line priced quantity list; primary
  output of the Ethical Estimation Engine).
- **CBR** — California Bearing Ratio (subgrade strength %; dominant road-cost
  driver in solar farm estimates).
- **DNO** — Distribution Network Operator (UK grid operator; grid-connection
  cost comes only from a DNO quote, never modelled).
- **IDB** — Internal Drainage Board (UK land-drainage authority; consent is
  an 8–12 week critical-path item on solar sites).
- **Free-issue** — equipment supplied by the client and installed by the EPC
  contractor; first-class input in the Estimation Engine.
- **GEOALT** — X,Y,Z terrain point-grid format ingested by the Estimation
  Engine's 3D terrain viewer.
- **PVcase** — commercial solar-farm design tool; its CSV/DXF exports can
  override the engine's derived quantities.
- **RE2020** — French environmental regulation for new buildings; basis for
  COREPROMA's carbon-grading thresholds.
- **ADEME** — French environment agency; source of the monetary carbon-
  intensity coefficients used by COREPROMA's and Jobs Tracker's carbon
  modules.
